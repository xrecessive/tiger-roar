# Traveling Tiger Center — Raja's Roar

A first-person tiger action game built from the real Traveling Tiger Center at
I-10 Exit 87, Sierra Blanca, TX. Everything is generated procedurally in Godot.

Play it: https://xrecessive.github.io/tiger-roar/

Click the canvas, then use WASD to move, the mouse to look, and left click or J
to roar. Shift sprints, Space jumps, Esc pauses, H shows the field guide, and
F11 toggles fullscreen. Collect roar upgrades, speed boosts, shields and heals.
